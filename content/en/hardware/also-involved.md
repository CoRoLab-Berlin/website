Projects by Festo AG in which we contributed to the control and fluidic-muscle drive.

## CyberKite (2009–2011)

A Festo AG funded kite system with cybernetic control unit and future-oriented mechatronic system that uses intelligent control technology to tame the forces of nature. Ivo Boblan was part of the control team.

## YoYo (2008)

Three 2 m large yoyo plays, each driven by one fluidic muscle.

## [Aqua_ray](https://www.festo.com/group/de/cms/10246.htm) (2007)

A manta ray driven by water hydraulics. The central drive and control unit for the wing and tail takes the form of a fluidic muscle from Festo, in combination with the Fin Ray Effect®.
