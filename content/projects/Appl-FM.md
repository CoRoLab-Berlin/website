The interdisciplinary BHT research program entitled "[Berlin Initiative for Applied Foundation Model Research (Appl-FM)](https://www.bht-berlin.de/4735)" is supported annually by the German Research Foundation (DFG) with one million euros to promote the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in various research areas such as robotics, biological research and healthcare.
