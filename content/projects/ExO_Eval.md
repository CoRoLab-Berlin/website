The [IFAF](https://www.ifaf-berlin.de/) funded project "[BackBooster-Exoskelett - Optimierung und Evaluation](https://www.ifaf-berlin.de/projekte/exo-eval/)" (ExO-Eval) is a joint project of:

- [Berlin University of Applied Sciences](http://www.bht-berlin.de) working group softrobotics of Prof. Dr.-Ing. Ivo W. Boblan,
- [Hochschule für Technik und Wirtschaft Berlin](https://www.htw-berlin.de/) working group medical physics of [Prof. Dr.-Ing. Sebastian Götz](https://www.htw-berlin.de/hochschule/personen/person/?eid=10974),
- [Carl Stahl Hebetechnik GmbH](https://www.carlstahl-hebetechnik.de/),
- [J. Schmalz GmbH](https://www.schmalz.com/de-de/),
- [Institut für Arbeitsschutz](https://www.dguv.de/ifa/index.jsp) bei der Deutschen gesetzlichen Unfallversicherung DGUV,
- [alfred rexroth GmbH](https://alfred-rexroth.de/),
- [Ernst Augustin e.K.](https://www.augustin-pumpen.de/),
- [Oxington GmbH](https://oxington.com/de/), and
- [Berlin Partner für Wirtschaft und Technologie GmbH](https://www.berlin-partner.de/).

In the ExO-Eval project, the passive exoskeleton BackBooster (formerly BiegeExo) developed at BHT and HTW is optimized and adapted in terms of ergonomics and support performance with the company J.Schmalz. A meaningful evaluation process is being developed and verified with Carl Stahl, enabling various exoskeletons to be measured optically (motion capturing), myoelectrically (EMG), and haptically (ground reaction forces) in the Human.VR.Lab at BHT. The results are prepared in such a way that a statement about their effectiveness can be made. Work processes, recreated in the Human.VR.Lab and at real workplaces, are measured with the practice partners, analyzed, and evaluated using the IFA.

The lab is responsible for designing the construction and the modeling, control, and supervision of the pneumatic drives for the exoskeleton.
