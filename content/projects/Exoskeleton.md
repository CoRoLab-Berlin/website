The BMWi funded project pneumatic muscle driven Exoskeleton (pExo) is a joint project of the companies and universities:

- [sachs holding GmbH](https://sachs-engineering.com/),
- [Meyer-Hayoz Design Engineering](https://www.meyer-hayoz.com),
- [TU Chemnitz Professorship for Assembly and Design Handling Technology (MHT)](https://www.tu-chemnitz.de/mb/MHT/index.php), and
- [Berlin University of Applied Sciences](https://projekt.bht-berlin.de/harmonik/forschung/exoskelett-zur-unterstuetzung-bmwi) working group softrobotics of Prof. Dr.-Ing. Ivo W. Boblan.

An interdisciplinary consortium from the fields of lightweight construction, ergonomics and design, kinematics and drive technology as well as control technology developed an exoskeleton as a lifting aid for physically demanding and monotonously repetitive activities. The human effort is reduced and transferred to the exoskeleton, relieving the wearer both physically and cognitively, allowing them to work with higher quality.
