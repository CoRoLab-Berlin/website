The acronym BROMMI stands for "Bionische Rüsselkinematik für sichere Roboteranwendungen in der Mensch-Maschine-Interaktion" in German. The TAK represents the demonstrator, which is entirely driven by fluidic muscles. TAK stands for "Tripedale Alternanzkaskade" in German.
