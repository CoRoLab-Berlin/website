The [BionicMotionRobot 2017 (BMR-4er)](https://www.festo.com/de/de/e/ueber-festo/forschung-und-entwicklung/bionic-learning-network/highlights-2015-2017/bionicmotionrobot-id_33307/) from the company Festo SE & Co. KG is a pneumatic lightweight robot with natural forms of movement. The BMR is inspired by the elephant's trunk and the tentacles of an octopus. The pneumatic lightweight robot has twelve degrees of freedom and can carry about three kilograms of payload relative to its own weight. For more information, you can view the [Brochure](https://www.festo.com/PDF_Flip/corp/Festo_BionicMotionRobot/de/index.html) or watch the [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GxVNTJinol8).

The BionicMotionRobot 2019 (BMB-2er) is a further development of the BMR-4er, which is only equipped with a pair of 2 bellows per segment. This concept formed the basis for the later [BionicSoftArm](https://www.festo.com/de/de/e/ueber-festo/forschung-und-entwicklung/bionic-learning-network/highlights-2018-2021/bionicsoftarm-id_68209/) from Festo.
