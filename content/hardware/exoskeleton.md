The [AiF](https://www.aif-projekt-gmbh.de/home.html) funded project Pneumatic Exoskeleton (pExo) is a joint project of the companies [sachs holding GmbH](https://sachs-engineering.com/), [Meyer-Hayoz Design Engineering](https://www.meyer-hayoz.com) and the universities TU Chemnitz [Professorship for Assembly and Design Handling Technology (MHT)](https://www.tu-chemnitz.de/mb/MHT/index.php) and the Berlin University of Applied Sciences (BHT), working group Compliant Robotics Lab (CoRoLab).
