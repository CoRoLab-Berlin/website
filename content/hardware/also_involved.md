- **CyberKite** (2009-2011, [pdf info](https://www.festo.com/net/de_group/SupportPortal/Downloads/146925), [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nhfFnEQM1aU)) is a Festo AG funded kite system with cybernetic control unit and future-oriented mechatronic system that uses intelligent control technology to tame the forces of nature. Ivo Boblan was part of the control team.
- **YoYo** (2008, [pdf info](https://www.festo.com/net/de_group/SupportPortal/Downloads/146939/42111/YoYo_de.pdf), [press release](https://www.festo.com/net/de_de/SupportPortal/Details/151004/PressArticle.aspx), [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5K15dV3K0rA)) are three 2m large yoyo plays each driven by one fluidic muscle.
- [**Aqua_ray**](https://www.festo.com/group/de/cms/10246.htm) (2007, [pdf info](https://www.festo.com/net/de_de/SupportPortal/Downloads/146913/42061/Aqua_ray_de.pdf), [press release](https://www.festo.com/net/de_de/SupportPortal/Details/151003/PressArticle.aspx), [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-vT-oidWyXE)) is a manta ray driven by water hydraulics. The central drive and control unit for the wing and tail takes the form of a fluidic muscle from Festo, in combination with the Fin Ray Effect®.
